* `lifecycle.prevent_destroy` now also protects a resource after it is removed from the configuration: `bundle deploy` records the flag in the deployment state, and `bundle deploy` and `bundle destroy` refuse to delete a resource whose last deployment had it set. To delete such a resource, add it back with `lifecycle.prevent_destroy: false`, deploy, then remove it again. ([#NNNN](https://github.com/databricks/cli/pull/NNNN))
