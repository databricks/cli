* `databricks air run --watch` and `databricks air logs --follow` now exit about 2 seconds sooner after a run ends: when the run's execution summary was the last log output, at least 5 seconds before the run finished, and later polls found nothing new, they stop at the first poll that sees the run finish instead of waiting for three more empty polls.
