* Fix `${resources...}` references to resource keys that start with a digit and contain a letter, such as `00_my_job`; a resource with such a key and `permissions` or `grants` failed to plan with `cannot parse`, and references to it were left unresolved. Keys that still cannot be referenced, those with no letter (`123`, `2024_01`, `_1`) or ending with `_` or `-` (`0job_`), are now rejected by `bundle validate` when the resource has `permissions` or `grants`. ([#7970](https://github.com/databricks/cli/pull/7970))
