* Add `lifecycle.bind` to resources to bring an existing workspace resource under bundle management at deploy time instead of creating a new one. The first deploy adopts the resource (`bind`) and applies the configuration if it differs (`bind_and_update`); while the block is present, deploys cannot recreate the resource, and `bundle destroy` still deletes it. ([#4630](https://github.com/databricks/cli/pull/4630), [#7366](https://github.com/databricks/cli/pull/7366))
