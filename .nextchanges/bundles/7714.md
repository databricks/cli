* `bundle validate` and `bundle summary` no longer include `current_user.roles` or `current_user.entitlements`. In `current_user`, `name` now holds the given and family names, `displayName` is those names joined with a space, `emails` holds the user name, `active` comes from the account status, and `groups` lists the transitive parent groups with only `value` and `display`. ([#7714](https://github.com/databricks/cli/pull/7714))
