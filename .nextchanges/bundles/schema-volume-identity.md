* Fixed `bundle destroy` deleting a schema or volume that someone else created under the name of the bundle's own after it was renamed out of band, in the direct deployment engine. Plan and deploy no longer take such an object for the bundle's, and destroy deletes the bundle's renamed schema or volume instead of leaving it behind. A deploy that removes such a renamed schema or volume from the config now deletes it at its new name too (a schema with its contents); before, it only dropped it from the state. A schema or volume deleted and created again outside the bundle is no longer the bundle's either: deploy fails on its name and destroy leaves it, until it is bound again with `bundle deployment unbind` and `bundle deployment bind`. ([#8056](https://github.com/databricks/cli/pull/8056))
