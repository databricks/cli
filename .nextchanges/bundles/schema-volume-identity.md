* Fixed `bundle destroy` deleting a schema or volume that someone else created under the name of the bundle's own after it was renamed out of band; plan and deploy no longer take it for the bundle's, and destroy deletes the bundle's renamed schema or volume instead of leaving it behind, in the direct deployment engine. A schema or volume deleted and created again outside the bundle is likewise no longer the bundle's: deploy fails on its name and destroy leaves it, until it is bound again with `bundle deployment unbind` and `bundle deployment bind`.
