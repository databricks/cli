* Fixed the direct deployment engine taking a schema or volume that someone else created under the name of the bundle's own, after it was renamed out of band, for the bundle's: `bundle deploy` no longer updates it and `bundle destroy` no longer deletes it; destroy drops the bundle's entry from the state and warns. A schema or volume deleted and created again outside the bundle is no longer the bundle's either: deploy fails on its name and destroy leaves it, until it is bound again with `bundle deployment unbind` and `bundle deployment bind`. ([#8056](https://github.com/databricks/cli/pull/8056))
