* Fixed `bundle destroy` in the direct deployment engine leaving behind a schema or volume renamed out of band: it now finds it by its `schema_id` or `volume_id` and deletes it at its new name. A deploy that removes such a schema or volume from the config deletes it there too (a schema with its contents); before, it only dropped it from the state. The resources in a renamed schema are deleted at their new name, or listed as going with the schema's delete, so approval and `lifecycle.prevent_destroy` still apply to them. ([#8096](https://github.com/databricks/cli/pull/8096))
