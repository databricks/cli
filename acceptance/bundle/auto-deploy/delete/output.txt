
=== Without a terminal, delete needs --auto-approve

>>> musterr [CLI] bundle auto-deploy delete
Error: this deletes the auto-deploy trigger of target dev. To proceed, use --auto-approve

=== Deleting the target's trigger leaves the other target's

>>> [CLI] bundle auto-deploy delete --auto-approve
Deleted the auto-deploy trigger of target dev.
