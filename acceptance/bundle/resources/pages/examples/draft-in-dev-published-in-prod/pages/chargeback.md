# Chargeback

A **chargeback** is a reversal of a card payment. It is initiated by the bank of the
cardholder, not by the merchant. Compare with a refund, which the merchant initiates.

## How we count it

| Metric            | Definition                                             |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| Chargeback count  | Number of chargebacks received in the period           |
| Chargeback rate   | Chargeback count divided by the number of card payments |

A chargeback is counted on the day the bank notifies us, not on the day of the payment.
