
=== Deploy with two ACLs
>>> [CLI] bundle deploy -qq

>>> acls
{"permission":"MANAGE","principal":"[USERNAME]"}
{"permission":"READ","principal":"deco-test-user@databricks.com"}
{"permission":"WRITE","principal":"users"}

=== Empty the list: an update down to the deploying user, not a delete
>>> [CLI] bundle plan
update secret_scopes.my_scope.permissions

Plan: 0 to add, 1 to change, 0 to delete, 1 unchanged

>>> [CLI] bundle deploy -qq

>>> acls
{"permission":"MANAGE","principal":"[USERNAME]"}
