=== Rename the schema. UC moves the model and the secret with it; they cannot follow a rename yet, so they are recreated where they moved

>>> update_file.py databricks.yml test-schema- test-schema-renamed-

>>> [CLI] bundle plan
recreate registered_models.model1
update schemas.schema1
recreate secrets.secret1

Plan: 2 to add, 1 to change, 2 to delete, 0 unchanged
