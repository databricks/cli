=== Removing the job from the config: deploy and destroy refuse to delete it

>>> [CLI] bundle plan
delete jobs.foo

Plan: 0 to add, 0 to change, 1 to delete, 0 unchanged

>>> musterr [CLI] bundle deploy
Uploading bundle files to /Workspace/Users/[USERNAME]/.bundle/dms-prevent-destroy-[UNIQUE_NAME]/default/files...
Error: resources.jobs.foo was removed from the configuration, but its last deployment has lifecycle.prevent_destroy set. To delete it, add it back with lifecycle.prevent_destroy: false and deploy, then remove it again

Files: 1 uploaded, 1 deleted

>>> musterr [CLI] bundle destroy --auto-approve
Error: resources.jobs.foo was removed from the configuration, but its last deployment has lifecycle.prevent_destroy set. To delete it, add it back with lifecycle.prevent_destroy: false and deploy, then remove it again
