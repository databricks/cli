
=== Selecting a child includes its binding dependency
>>> [CLI] bundle plan --select child -o json

>>> jq {bind: (.bind | keys), plan: (.plan | keys), not_selected} tmp.plan.json
{
  "bind": [
    "resources.jobs.parent"
  ],
  "plan": [
    "resources.jobs.child",
    "resources.jobs.parent"
  ],
  "not_selected": 1
}
